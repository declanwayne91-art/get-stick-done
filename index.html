TO-DO LISTS

LISTS THAT WORK THE WAY YOU DO.

A long to-do list is hard to face. So create a list for anything, break the big jobs down into little steps and earn a sticker for every little win. Share lists with your friends and team up to get St*ck Done together!

  • Make your own lists for shopping, packing, chores or anything else you need to get done
MEALS

A WEEK OF DINNERS IN A FEW TAPS.

Deciding what to eat every night is tiring. So plan the whole week in a few taps, or let the app choose for you. Your shopping list is written for you from the week’s recipes, sorted by aisle and ready to tick off in the shop.

  • Cook along one step at a time, with built-in timers
  • Tell the app what’s in your fridge and it finds recipes that use up those ingredients
  • 77+ recipes, from smash burger tacos and Marry Me chicken to hot honey halloumi wraps, with allergen labels and tips for feeding little ones
every dish has its own sticker
WORKOUTS

MOVE YOUR WAY.

Not every day is a gym day, so pick what suits you: a gym session, walk, run, cycle, swim or stretch. Gym sessions take you through each exercise set by set, with how-to guides and rest timers. The app saves your progress, so you can build up bit by bit and your goals feel achievable.

HOBBIES

TIME FOR YOU.

Hobbies count as getting St*ck done too. Tick off the days you read, and every book you finish goes on your library shelf. Keep track of the music you’re learning and the games you’re playing, with challenges that earn stickers. Friends can see what you’re into and cheer you on.
