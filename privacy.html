GET ST*CK DONE!

PRIVACY

Last updated 11 November 2026

The short version. Your tasks, meals, workouts and sticker book are saved on your own phone. The app has no accounts, so it never asks for your email address or a password. Our servers only store something when you add friends, share something with them, or switch on reminders. There are no adverts and we never sell your information.

WHO WE ARE

Get St*ck Done! is made by Dex, an independent developer in the United Kingdom. For anything on this page, email hello@getstickdone.app.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR PHONE

These are saved in the app on your phone. They only leave it if you choose one of the sharing or reminder features described in the next section:

  • your own tasks and lists
  • your meal plan, shopping list and the recipes you cook
  • your workouts, the weights you lift and your progress
  • how much water you drink
  • your hobbies, books and games
  • your sticker book and pin badges

Because this lives on your phone, deleting the app or clearing its storage removes it, and we have no copy to restore.

WHAT OUR SERVERS STORE, AND WHEN

Each of these only happens if you use that feature.

If you add friends. We store the name you typed in the app, your friend code, your name plate colour, your level, your favourite sticker and pin badge, how many stickers and pin badges you have, and anything you have set as reading, playing or learning. Your friends see these on your friend card. We also store who you are friends with and who you have blocked. Your phone is recognised by a random secret made on the phone. We store only a scrambled version of it, which cannot be turned back into the original.

If you send a cheer, share a dinner or invite friends to a game night. We store that message so your friend can see it. Once it has been seen, it is deleted after 30 days.

If you share a list. We store the list’s name, the tasks on it, who is on the list and who ticked what. Lists you don’t share stay on your phone. A shared list is deleted when the last person leaves it.

If you share your week’s meals. We store the dinners in that week, the shopping items that are ticked and any extras you added. It is deleted when the last person stops sharing.

If you switch on reminders. To send a notification we store your phone’s notification address, the times you chose and your time zone. For water reminders we also store today’s glass count and your goal, so we don’t remind you after you’ve reached it. For gym reminders we store which days you chose and the name of that day’s workout. Switching a reminder off deletes this.

If you move to a new phone. MOVE TO A NEW PHONE scrambles everything on your old phone using a code that only your two phones know, and leaves that scrambled copy on our server so the new phone can collect it. We cannot read it. It is deleted as soon as the new phone has it, or after 10 minutes if it is never collected.

ANONYMOUS USAGE COUNTS

To learn which parts of the app are useful, the app sends simple counts, such as “a task was ticked off” or “a workout was finished”, with the date and the app version. They are tied to a random number that is not linked to your name, your friend code or anything you typed. Nothing you type is ever included. Counts are kept for 13 months.

You can switch this off in the app under Me, then the settings cog. Switching it off also deletes the counts your phone has already sent.

THE TESTER GROUP AND EMAIL

If you join our tester group on Google Groups, we can see the Google account email address you joined with. We only use it to give you access to the test on Google Play and to email the group about the test. You can leave the group at any time, which removes you from it.

If you email us, we keep your message and address so we can reply.

WHAT WE DON’T DO

  • No adverts and no advertising trackers.
  • We never sell or rent your information.
  • No cookies on this website. The app saves your data in your phone’s storage so it works; that is not used to track you.
  • We don’t ask for your location, contacts, photos or camera.

COMPANIES THAT HELP US RUN IT

These companies process data for us so the app and website can work:

  • Cloudflare runs the servers and database described above, and may store data outside the United Kingdom.
  • GitHub hosts this website and the app’s files. Like any web host, it sees the internet address of devices that load them.
  • Google supplies the fonts, which your device loads from Google, and runs Google Play, Google Groups and our email.
  • Your phone’s notification service (Google for Android and Chrome, Apple for iPhone) delivers reminders and friend alerts. They are encrypted so that service cannot read them.

DELETING YOUR INFORMATION

  • Everything on your phone: in the app, go to Me, tap the settings cog and choose START FRESH. This wipes the app on that phone, switches off its reminders and friend alerts on our servers, and deletes your friend profile.
  • Just your friend profile: in the app, open Friends, tap INVITE A FRIEND and choose “delete my friend profile”. This deletes your name plate, friend code, friends, blocks and cheers from our servers straight away, and takes you out of any shared lists and shared meals.
  • If you no longer have the app: email hello@getstickdone.app with your friend code and we will delete your friend profile, shared lists and shared meals within 30 days.
  • Usage counts: switch them off in Me, then the settings cog.

If you stop using the app without doing any of this, a friend profile stays on our servers until you ask us to remove it.

YOUR RIGHTS

Under UK data protection law you can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to how we use it. Email hello@getstickdone.app and we will reply within one month. We rely on your consent for reminders, on providing the features you ask for when you use friends and sharing, and on our legitimate interest in improving the app for anonymous usage counts.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
